Why is a tree appropriate for the doctor structure?

A tree is useful since it represents the hierarchical structure of the doctors. 
It makes it easy to represent who is above or below who in the corporate hierarchy.
It also makes it more obvious who someone answers to if that information is necessary.
In general, it is one of the closest representations to the actual structure of the doctors.


When might a software engineer use preorder, inorder, or postorder traversals?

Preorder is useful for copying a tree or any operation where the root significantly impacts the operation.
Inorder is useful for sorted data due to how it traverses the tree, resulting in sorted output.
Postorder is useful in opposite cases to preorder, where the leaves greatly impact the result.


How do heaps help simulate real-time systems like emergency intake?

The automatic reordering of a heap represents how these systems will constantly shift to ensure the most important information is first.
This is useful for real-time systems such as the emergency intake in this assignment, but also when prioritizing tasks in any organization.
Heaps simulate these systems well when the things involved have different priorities that need to be accounted for.
